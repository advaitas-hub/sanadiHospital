PATIENT QUESTIONS

Questions Patients Often Ask

Do I need a referral to consult a specialist at Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital?

No. You can book a direct consultation with any of our orthopaedic, trauma, or neurosurgery specialists without a referral.

Is robotic-assisted joint replacement suitable for every patient?

Not necessarily. The appropriate treatment depends on your joint condition, medical history and assessment by the treating specialist.

What happens if I arrive with a trauma emergency outside OPD hours?

Our emergency and trauma care team is available 24×7 for fracture stabilization, polytrauma response, and emergency surgery — no appointment needed.

When can I start walking after joint replacement?

The timing varies from patient to patient and depends on the type of surgery, overall health and the treating surgeon's advice.

How can I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment through the hospital's appointment process or by contacting the hospital directly.
