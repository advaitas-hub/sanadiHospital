OUR BEGINNING • EST. 2003

Built in Jamkhandi. Growing with the Community.

Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital began in Jamkhandi in 2003 with a simple purpose — to make dependable orthopaedic care available closer to home. Over the years, the hospital has grown in experience, facilities and clinical expertise, while remaining connected to the people and communities it serves.

From everyday bone and joint conditions to emergency trauma management, our focus has remained fixed on providing clear medical guidance, patient comfort, and trustworthy care for families across Jamkhandi and North Karnataka.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Understand first. Treat thoughtfully. Stay with you through recovery.

At Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital, we believe meaningful healthcare begins with listening. Every patient comes with a unique condition, personal concerns, and distinct recovery goals that deserve careful attention.

We take the time to explain your diagnosis clearly, discuss non-surgical and surgical options openly, and involve you and your family in deciding the right path forward.

Care does not end with a procedure — rehabilitation, pain management, and attentive follow-up visits are an essential part of helping you regain comfort and mobility.

OUR JOURNEY

Two Decades of Dedicated Care

From our founding in 2003 to our modern hospital facility today, explore the evolution of Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital.

OUR ORIGIN

2003

Where it began.

In 2003, Dr. G. N. Sanadi started the hospital with a focus on orthopaedic care. What began as a commitment to serve patients in Jamkhandi has grown into a hospital caring for people from Jamkhandi, Bagalkot and nearby communities. The journey has been shaped by the needs of the people who come here — from those seeking treatment for an injury to patients working towards recovery after surgery.

OUR VISION

Good orthopaedic care, closer to home.

Our aim is to make dependable orthopaedic care accessible to people in our region, so patients can receive the treatment, guidance and follow-up they need without always having to travel far from home.

OUR MISSION

Patient-centred treatment & recovery.

Providing accurate diagnosis, thoughtful treatment choices, and steady support throughout every patient's recovery journey.

TWO DECADES LATER

Expanding capabilities, preserving personal care.

Over twenty years since our doors first opened, Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital has expanded into a multi-specialty orthopaedic center with dedicated operating theatres, ICU facilities, inpatient beds, and diagnostic support. While our facilities have grown, our core focus remains unchanged.

Today, we continue to combine modern surgical practices with personal, attentive care — ensuring every patient receives the focused treatment they need right here in Jamkhandi.
