Clinical Overview

Understanding Physical Rehabilitation

Recovery does not end after surgery or treatment. Physiotherapy helps restore movement, strength, flexibility, and confidence so you can get back to your everyday activities.

At Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital, our physiotherapy team provides personalised rehabilitation programs for patients recovering from surgery, injuries, joint problems, and long-term pain.

Your treatment plan is based on your condition, mobility, and recovery needs, with exercises and therapy adjusted as you progress.

Symptom Evaluation

When stiffness and weakness hinder recovery

Physiotherapy can help when pain, stiffness, weakness, or difficulty moving begins to affect your everyday life.

01

Stiffness After Surgery

Difficulty moving a joint normally after joint replacement, fracture treatment, or other surgery.

02

Muscle Weakness

Loss of strength after an injury, surgery, prolonged rest, or reduced physical activity.

03

Difficulty Walking

Limping, poor balance, or difficulty walking comfortably due to pain, weakness, or joint problems.

04

Ongoing Back Pain

Persistent back pain or stiffness that affects your posture, movement, work, or daily activities.

05

Frozen Shoulder & Limited Movement

Pain and stiffness that make it difficult to raise your arm, reach overhead, or perform everyday tasks.

Rehab Protocol

Your Physical Therapy Pathway

A gradual, personalised approach designed to help you safely regain movement and independence.

01

Assessment

Your movement, strength, flexibility, balance, and pain levels are assessed to understand your rehabilitation needs.

02

Pain Management

Appropriate therapy is used to help manage pain, swelling, and stiffness.

03

Mobility Exercises

Gentle exercises and guided movement help improve flexibility and joint mobility.

04

Strengthening

Progressive exercises help rebuild muscle strength, balance, and joint support.

05

Functional Recovery

Your therapy progresses toward everyday activities such as walking, climbing stairs, and managing tasks independently.
