Clinical Overview

Understanding Physical Rehabilitation

Surgery or prolonged joint immobilization is only the first step toward recovery; restoring full muscle strength, joint flexibility, and fluid gait dynamics requires customized, supervised physical therapy.

At Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital, our physical therapy center is equipped with advanced electrotherapy modalities, parallel walking bars, muscle stimulators, and joint mobilization equipment operated by experienced physical therapists.

We work in direct coordination with operating surgeons to ensure every patient receives a safe, progressive rehabilitation plan matched precisely to their post-surgical healing timeline.

Symptom Evaluation

When stiffness and weakness hinder recovery

Key mobility challenges that respond effectively to specialized physical therapy.

01

Post-Surgical Joint Stiffness

Reduced joint range of motion following joint replacement, fracture casting, or ligament repair surgery.

02

Muscle Atrophy & Weakness

Visible quadriceps or calf muscle shrinking after immobilizing casts or prolonged bed rest.

03

Altered Gait & Limping Walk

Compensatory walking limp resulting from hip pain, knee stiffness, or ankle imbalance.

04

Chronic Musculoskeletal Back Pain

Persistent lower back aching caused by weak core muscles, postural strain, or spinal disc degeneration.

05

Frozen Shoulder & Stiffness

Severe restriction in arm lifting and reaching due to painful shoulder adhesive capsulitis.

Rehab Programs

Comprehensive Rehabilitation Services

Specialized physical therapy programs tailored to your specific recovery goals.

01 / Post-Surgical Rehab

Joint Replacement & Fracture Rehabilitation

Structured post-operative protocols guiding patients through knee bending, stair climbing, and unassisted walking starting from Day 1.

  • Continuous Passive Motion (CPM) Knee Flexion
  • Parallel Bar Balance & Gait Retraining
  • Muscle Re-education & Strengthening
02 / Pain Management

Electrotherapy & Pain Modalities

Advanced Interferential Therapy (IFT), TENS, Therapeutic Ultrasound, and Deep Tissue Mobilization for rapid inflammation and pain relief.

  • Digital IFT & TENS Pain Management
  • Therapeutic Ultrasound & Phonophoresis
  • Deep Tissue Myofascial Release
03 / Spine & Core Care

Spinal Ergonomics & Core Conditioning

Targeted lumbar spine stabilization exercises, cervical posture correction, and ergonomic work-station training for disc back pain.

  • Core Muscle Deep Stabilization
  • Cervical & Lumbar Traction Therapy
  • Ergonomic Posture Correction Guidance
Rehab Protocol

Your Physical Therapy Pathway

01

Gait Evaluation

Pain Relief
