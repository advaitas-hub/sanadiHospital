Clinical Overview

Understanding Sports Medicine

Athletic performance demands peak musculoskeletal endurance. When acute tears or chronic overuse injuries occur, athletes require rapid diagnostic turnaround and high-performance recovery protocols.

The Sports Injury Clinic at Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital combines minimally invasive arthroscopic repair, ultrasound-guided biological injections (PRP), and dynamic biomechanical conditioning.

Our objective is not just pain elimination, but returning athletes to peak competitive condition with restored joint stability, explosive power, and reduced re-injury risk.

Symptom Evaluation

When athletic performance is compromised

Common sports-related injuries and mechanical symptoms requiring specialized athletic evaluation.

01

Acute Ligament Tears (ACL / PCL / MCL)

Sudden knee joint collapse, severe swelling, and inability to continue playing sports following a sudden pivot or tackle.

02

Recurrent Ankle Sprains & Instability

Frequent twisting of the ankle on uneven surfaces caused by stretched or torn lateral ankle ligaments.

03

Chronic Tendonitis (Achilles & Patellar)

Persistent heel or knee tendon pain during running or jumping that limits training intensity.

04

Throwing Shoulder Impingement

Sharp pain when throwing a ball, serving in tennis, or swimming due to labral tear or rotator cuff strain.

05

Muscle Strains & Hamstring Tears

Sudden tearing pain in the hamstring, groin, or calf muscle during high-speed sprinting.

Clinical Expertise

Comprehensive Sports Injury Care

Advanced medical and biological treatments focused on athletic restoration.

01 / Athletic Ligament Care

Keyhole ACL/PCL Repair & Anatomic Reconstruction

Minimally invasive autograft ligament reconstruction restoring rotational joint stability for contact and pivoting sports.

  • Anatomical Single/Double-Bundle Ligament Reconstruction
  • Lateral Extra-Articular Tenodesis (LET) Support
  • Accelerated Athletic Rehabilitation Protocol
02 / Biological Injections

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) Biological Therapy

Ultrasound-guided autologous growth factor injections accelerating natural healing in chronic Achilles tendonitis, tennis elbow, and patellar tendinopathy.

  • Ultrasound-Guided Targeted Infiltration
  • High-Concentration Autologous Growth Factors
  • Non-Surgical Tendon Repair Protocol
03 / Return-To-Play Clearance

Biomechanical Testing & Sports Clearance

Objective force-plate limb symmetry testing, agility drills, and psychological readiness screening prior to clearing athletes for competitive match play.

  • Limb Symmetry Index (LSI) Testing
  • Sport-Specific Agility & Deceleration Drills
  • Certified Sports Clearance Certificate
Athletic Protocol

Your Return-To-Play Pathway

A structured performance clinical pipeline designed to safely get you back on the field.

01

Injury Exam

Immediate clinical evaluation, ligament stress testing, and joint stability check.

02

3T MRI Scan

High-contrast soft tissue MRI imaging mapping exact muscle and tendon tear severity.

03

Targeted Care

Biological PRP infiltration or keyhole arthroscopic ligament reconstruction.

04

Conditioning

Supervised strength, plyometric jumping, and agility retraining.

05

Match Clearance

Objective limb symmetry testing and official return-to-play sports clearance.
