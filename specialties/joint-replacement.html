Clinical Overview

Understanding Joint Replacement

When severe joint damage from osteoarthritis, rheumatoid disease, or trauma compromises daily movement, joint replacement surgery offers a proven pathway back to a active, pain-free life.

At Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital, joint replacement is executed with computer-assisted precision. By preserving healthy surrounding soft tissue and selecting biocompatible titanium and ceramic implants customized to your anatomy, our surgical team minimizes post-operative discomfort and accelerates your return to daily activities.

Each patient undergoes comprehensive pre-surgical evaluation and functional mapping to ensure the chosen joint component provides optimal alignment, natural range of motion, and decades of reliable durability.

Symptom Evaluation

When movement starts becoming difficult

Key joint symptoms and functional limitations indicating it is time to consult an arthroplasty specialist.

01

Severe Chronic Joint Pain

Persistent aching during walking, standing, or resting that fails to respond to oral medications and physical therapy.

02

Progressive Stiffness & Reduced Flexibility

Difficulty bending your knee, tying shoes, climbing stairs, or getting up from a low chair independently.

03

Bone-on-Bone Friction & Crepitus

Audible popping, clicking, or grating sensations caused by severe cartilage erosion within the joint capsule.

04

Joint Deformity & Instability

Visible bowing of the legs (bow-leg or knock-knee alignment) or sudden joint buckling during normal walking.

05

Loss of Daily Independence

Inability to take routine walks, travel with family, or enjoy personal hobbies due to persistent mobility fatigue.

Total & Partial Knee Replacement

Computer-navigated knee resurfacing designed to reconstruct worn cartilage surfaces with ultra-smooth femoral and tibial implants, matching your natural gait dynamics.

02 / Hip Arthroplasty

Total & Revision Hip Replacement

Direct muscle-preserving surgical approach replacing damaged hip sockets and femoral heads with wear-resistant ceramic components for rapid weight-bearing recovery.

  • Direct Muscle-Sparing Surgical Access
  • Ultra-Low Friction Ceramic Bearings
  • Day 1 Accelerated Weight-Bearing Protocol
03 / Shoulder Arthroplasty

Reverse & Total Shoulder Replacement

Specialized shoulder joint reconstruction for complex cuff tear arthropathy and severe glenohumeral arthritis, restoring pain-free overhead arm elevation.
