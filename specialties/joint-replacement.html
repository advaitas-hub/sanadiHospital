Clinical Overview

Understanding Joint Replacement

When joint damage causes persistent pain and makes everyday activities difficult, joint replacement may be considered when other treatments no longer provide enough relief.

At Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital, we provide personalised joint replacement care for conditions affecting the knee, hip, and shoulder. Your treatment begins with a detailed evaluation to understand your symptoms, joint condition, and mobility needs.

When surgery is recommended, your doctor will explain the procedure, implant options, expected recovery, and rehabilitation process so you can make an informed decision.

Symptom Evaluation

When movement starts becoming difficult

Persistent joint pain and stiffness can gradually affect your mobility and independence. Consider an orthopaedic evaluation if you experience:

01

Persistent Joint Pain

Pain that continues during walking, standing, climbing stairs, or everyday activities despite other treatments.

02

Stiffness & Reduced Movement

Difficulty bending or straightening your knee, moving your hip, or using your shoulder comfortably.

03

Joint Grinding or Clicking

Grinding, clicking, or other unusual sensations in a joint, especially when accompanied by pain or stiffness.

04

Joint Deformity or Instability

Changes in joint alignment or a feeling that your knee, hip, or shoulder may give way during movement.

05

Difficulty With Everyday Activities

Joint problems that make walking, travelling, climbing stairs, exercising, or enjoying your usual activities difficult.
