Clinical Overview

Understanding Keyhole Arthroscopy

Arthroscopy has revolutionized joint care by allowing surgeons to inspect, diagnose, and repair internal ligament and cartilage tears through tiny buttonhole incisions without opening the joint capsule.

At Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital, our keyhole surgical suite features high-definition 4K camera systems and specialized micro-instruments. This allows our surgeons to reconstruct torn ACL ligaments or repair meniscus cartilage with extreme anatomical fidelity.

Because healthy surrounding tissues are left untouched, patients experience significantly reduced post-operative pain, minimal scarring, and rapid progression into active rehabilitation.

Symptom Evaluation

When joint injury limits your active life

Key ligament and cartilage symptoms indicating the need for keyhole arthroscopic repair.

01

Audible Pop & Sudden Joint Buckling

Feeling a sudden pop in the knee during sports or twisting, followed by immediate instability and joint giving way.

02

Joint Locking & Meniscus Catching

Inability to fully straighten or flex your knee because torn cartilage fragments are physically blocking joint motion.

03

Recurrent Shoulder Dislocation

Frequent slipping out of the shoulder joint during overhead reaching, sleeping, or athletic throwing movements.

04

Persistent Overhead Arm Weakness

Inability to lift your arm overhead without sharp shoulder pain, pointing toward a rotator cuff tendon tear.

05

Chronic Post-Traumatic Joint Swelling

Recurrent fluid accumulation inside the knee or ankle after physical exercise or long walks.

Keyhole Protocol

Your Keyhole Recovery Pathway

From precise 4K camera diagnostic mapping to complete athletic readiness.

01

Clinical Check

02

MRI Mapping

High-contrast 3T MRI mapping of internal meniscus and ligament tear locations.

03

Graft Selection

Athletic Rehab
