Clinical Overview

Understanding Keyhole Arthroscopy

Arthroscopy is a minimally invasive procedure that allows doctors to examine and treat problems inside a joint through small incisions.

At Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital, arthroscopy is used to treat a range of knee, shoulder, ankle, and elbow conditions, including ligament injuries, cartilage damage, and joint instability.

Depending on your condition, keyhole surgery may help reduce tissue damage and support a smoother recovery compared with some traditional surgical approaches.

Symptom Evaluation

When joint injury limits your active life

Joint injuries can affect your movement, work, exercise, and everyday activities. Consider an orthopaedic evaluation if you experience:

01

Sudden Knee Pain or Instability

A sudden pop, pain, or feeling that your knee may give way after a sports injury or twisting movement.

02

Joint Locking or Catching

Difficulty fully bending or straightening your knee, or a feeling that the joint is catching during movement.

03

Repeated Shoulder Dislocation

A shoulder that repeatedly slips out of place or feels unstable during everyday activities or exercise.

04

Shoulder Pain & Weakness

Persistent shoulder pain or weakness, especially when lifting your arm or reaching overhead.

05

Persistent Joint Swelling

Repeated swelling or discomfort in the knee, ankle, elbow, or other joint, particularly after activity or injury.

Keyhole Protocol

Your Keyhole Recovery Pathway

A clear process to help you understand your injury, treatment options, and recovery.

01

Clinical Assessment

Your symptoms, joint movement, strength, and stability are carefully evaluated.

02

Imaging & Diagnosis

Appropriate imaging and diagnostic tests are recommended to understand the extent of the injury.

03

Treatment Planning

Your doctor explains the available treatment options and recommends the most suitable approach for your condition.

04

Keyhole Surgery

If surgery is required, the damaged joint structures are treated through a minimally invasive arthroscopic approach.

05

Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy and guided exercises help restore movement, strength, stability, and function.
