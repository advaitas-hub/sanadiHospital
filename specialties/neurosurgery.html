Clinical Overview

Understanding Spine & Neuro Care

Your spine and nervous system play an important role in movement, balance, sensation, and everyday activities. Problems affecting the spine or nerves can cause pain, numbness, weakness, and difficulty moving.

At Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital, our spine and neurosurgery team provides careful evaluation and personalised treatment for a range of spinal and neurological conditions.

Depending on your condition, treatment may include medicines, physiotherapy, injections, or surgery when necessary, with the goal of relieving symptoms and helping you return to your daily activities.

Symptom Evaluation

When spinal pain limits your mobility

Persistent back or neck pain should not be ignored, especially when it is accompanied by changes in sensation, strength, or movement.

01

Radiating Leg Pain (Sciatica)

Pain that travels from your lower back through your buttock and leg, sometimes reaching the foot.

02

Numbness & Tingling

Pins-and-needles, numbness, or unusual sensations in your arms, hands, legs, or feet.

03

Weakness or Difficulty Moving

Weakness in an arm or leg, difficulty walking, frequent tripping, or reduced grip strength.

04

Leg Pain While Walking

Pain, heaviness, or weakness in your legs that gets worse when walking and improves with rest or sitting.

05

Neck & Arm Pain

Neck pain or stiffness that spreads into the shoulder, arm, or hand and may be accompanied by weakness or numbness.

Treatment options for spinal and neurological conditions, ranging from minimally invasive procedures to complex surgery when required.

Emergency Neurotrauma Care

Spine Protocol

Your Spine Care Pathway

A clear step-by-step approach to understanding your condition and choosing the right treatment.

01

Clinical Assessment

Your symptoms, medical history, movement, strength, and sensation are carefully evaluated.

02

Diagnosis & Imaging

Appropriate imaging and diagnostic tests are recommended to identify the cause of your symptoms.

03

Treatment Plan
