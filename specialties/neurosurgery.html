Clinical Overview

Understanding Spine & Neuro Care

The central nervous system and spine dictate every movement, sensation, and physical reflex. When spinal nerve roots suffer compression from herniated discs, chronic pain and limb weakness can impair daily life.

Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital's Neurosurgery Department utilizes state-of-the-art operative microscopes and endoscopic instrumentation to achieve nerve decompression through incisions smaller than an inch.

By protecting surrounding paraspinal muscles and preserving natural spinal motion, micro-endoscopic spine procedures significantly reduce recovery time, allowing patients to walk comfortably within hours of surgery.

Symptom Evaluation

When spinal pain limits your mobility

Key neurological symptoms indicating nerve root compression or spinal column distress.

01

Radiating Leg Pain (Sciatica)

Sharp, electric shock-like shooting pain extending from the lower back down through the buttock, thigh, and calf.

02

Limb Numbness & Tingling Sensations

Pins-and-needles numbness in feet, toes, or fingers caused by nerve root compression in the lumbar or cervical spine.

03

Progressive Motor Weakness (Foot Drop)

Inability to lift the front of the foot, frequent tripping, or loss of hand grip strength indicating advanced nerve distress.

04

Neurogenic Claudication

Heaviness or cramping in legs after walking short distances that subsides only when sitting or leaning forward.

05

Acute Cervical Neck & Arm Pain

Severe neck stiffness accompanied by radiating shoulder and hand weakness from cervical disc protrusion.

Surgical Procedures

Specialized Neurosurgery Capabilities

Minimally invasive micro-discectomy and complex spinal reconstruction procedures.

01 / Keyhole Spine Surgery

Microdiscectomy & Nerve Decompression

Microscopic removal of herniated disc fragments pressing against spinal nerve roots, offering instant relief from severe leg pain and sciatica.

  • Ultra-Small 18mm Skin Incision
  • High-Magnification Operative Microscope
  • Same-Day or 24-Hour Hospitalization
02 / Spinal Stabilization

Minimally Invasive Spinal Fusion (MIS-TLIF)

Pedicle screw and interbody cage placement for spinal instability, spondylolisthesis, and severe degenerative disc disease, restoring vertebral height.

  • Percutaneous Pedicle Screw Fixation
  • Intraoperative Neuromonitoring (IONM)
  • Preserved Paraspinal Muscle Integrity
03 / Brain & Head Trauma

Emergency Neurotrauma & Craniotomy

24/7 surgical evacuation of subdural and epidural hematomas, brain contusion decompression, and specialized Neuro-ICU monitoring following head trauma.

  • 24/7 Emergency Craniotomy Readiness
  • Intracranial Pressure (ICP) Monitoring
  • Dedicated Neuro-Intensive Care Unit
