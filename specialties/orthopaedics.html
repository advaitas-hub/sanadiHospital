Clinical Overview

Understanding Orthopaedic Care

The orthopaedic system forms the structural backbone of human mobility. When bones, joints, ligaments, or tendons experience degeneration or sudden trauma, expert medical intervention is essential.

At Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital, our general orthopaedics team emphasizes precise clinical diagnosis, evidence-based conservative management, and minimally disruptive surgical interventions when necessary.

Whether addressing age-related osteoarthritis, sudden bone fractures, or metabolic bone weakness, our holistic care pathways focus on preserving natural bone stock and restoring functional strength.

KNOW WHEN TO SEEK CARE

When should you see an orthopaedic specialist?

You don't have to ignore persistent pain or difficulty moving. If you're experiencing any of these problems, it may be time to have your bones, joints or muscles evaluated.

01

Ongoing Bone or Joint Pain

Pain that keeps coming back or gets worse when you walk, work, exercise or move.

02

Difficulty Moving

Stiffness or reduced movement in your knee, hip, shoulder, back or other joints.

03

Pain After an Injury

Pain, swelling or difficulty using an arm or leg after a fall, accident or sports injury.

04

Your Joint Feels Unstable

Your knee, ankle, shoulder or other joint feels like it may give way, slip or lock.

05

Changes in Strength or Movement

Increasing weakness, difficulty walking, changes in posture or trouble with everyday activities.

Clinical Expertise

Comprehensive Orthopaedic Care

Specialized diagnostic and therapeutic care programs tailored for long-term musculoskeletal health.

01 / Fracture Management

Fracture Fixation & Trauma Realignment

Rapid closed reduction, anatomical cast application, and internal fixation using titanium plates and intramedullary rods for complex bone fractures.

  • Rigid Internal Fixation Technology
  • Non-Disruptive Minimally Invasive Plating
  • Serial Digital X-Ray Union Monitoring
02 / Joint Preservation

Non-Surgical Joint & Cartilage Therapy

Targeted intra-articular viscosity supplementation, biological growth factor therapy, and custom knee unloading braces to delay or prevent joint surgery.

  • Viscosupplementation Hyaluronic Acid Injections
  • Biomechanical Gait Unloading
  • Targeted Cartilage Preservation Exercises
03 / Bone Density

Osteoporosis & Metabolic Bone Care

Comprehensive DEXA bone mineral density screening, metabolic calcium balance therapy, and specialized fragility fracture prevention programs.

  • DEXA Bone Density Scanning
  • Anti-Resorptive & Anabolic Medication Protocols
  • Fall Prevention & Core Strengthening
Structured Protocol

Your Care Journey

A transparent clinical workflow guiding you from initial assessment to bone union and recovery.

01

Consultation

Thorough clinical examination, palpation, and range-of-motion assessment with orthopedic specialists.

02

Diagnosis

Immediate digital radiography and high-resolution imaging to evaluate bone structure and alignment.

03

Treatment Plan

Evidence-based selection between conservative splinting/casting or surgical internal fixation.

04

Clinical Care

Precision stabilization or surgical realignment performed under sterile laminar airflow conditions.

05

Rehabilitation

Supervised physical therapy and progressive weight-bearing to ensure complete bone healing.
