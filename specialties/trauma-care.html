Clinical Overview

Understanding Emergency Trauma Care

In severe road accidents, falls, or industrial injuries, immediate medical stabilization within the "Golden Hour" determines patient survival and long-term limb function.

Sanadi Orthopaedic Hospital maintains a dedicated 24/7 Level-1 Trauma Care Facility with an on-call team of trauma surgeons, neurosurgeons, anaesthesiologists, and ICU critical care specialists ready to initiate damage control surgery instantly.

Our emergency department is seamlessly connected to digital X-ray, CT imaging, blood bank access, and fully sterile modular operating theatres to minimize delay in critical surgical interventions.

Emergency Indicators

Conditions Requiring Immediate Trauma Intervention

Urgent clinical presentations requiring immediate emergency department transport.

01

High-Impact Road Traffic Accidents

Polytrauma involving multiple bone fractures, pelvic injuries, and internal soft tissue disruption following vehicular collisions.

02

Open & Compound Fractures

Bone breaks where bone fragments breach the skin surface, presenting high contamination and infection risk requiring immediate debridement.

03

Joint Dislocations & Vascular Compromise

Severe joint dislodgement causing limb numbness, pale skin, or pulselessness requiring emergency reduction.

04

Acute Spine Trauma & Nerve Compression

Traumatic neck or back injury accompanied by limb weakness, numbness, or paralysis.

05

Crush Injuries & Compartment Syndrome

Severe muscle swelling and tissue pressure buildup following heavy object impact requiring emergency surgical fasciotomy.

Emergency Protocol

The 5-Stage Trauma Pathway

Rapid clinical response sequence designed to preserve life and limb function.

01

Immediate Triage

Immediate vital sign evaluation and airway stabilization upon emergency department arrival.

02

STAT Diagnostics

Rapid digital X-rays and CT scanning conducted within minutes of admission.

03

Resuscitation

Hemodynamic stabilization, fluid resuscitation, and acute pain management.

04

Damage Control OT

Urgent surgical realignment, debridement, or fracture fixator placement.

05

ICU & Recovery

Close post-operative monitoring transitioning to early rehabilitation therapy.
